Discovery call script · Rebuttal guide · Delivery process · Website formula

📋Before the call — prep (5 min)
  • Pull up their current website — have it open on your screen
  • Note their Google rating and number of reviews
  • Note the 2–3 specific site issues our system flagged (no HTTPS, no mobile, no booking, etc.)
  • Know their city and category
  • Have the before/after mockup email open so you can reference it
  • Have Calendly open — you may need to confirm the appointment time
  • Have the pricing ready: $1,500 website · $200–500 chatbot add-on
📞Discovery call script — 20 minutes

Opening (0–2 min)

"Hi, is this [Name]? Great — this is Alex from WebRevive AI. Thanks for grabbing some time with me today. I'll keep this short — I know you're running a business and your time is valuable.

Before I say anything else, I just want to confirm — did you get a chance to see the before and after mockup we sent over for [Business Name]?"
💡 Wait for their answer. If yes — great, build on it. If no — pull it up together: "No worries, let me walk you through it right now — it'll take 30 seconds."

The Hook (2–5 min)

"So what we do is pretty simple — we find local businesses with websites that are holding them back, and we rebuild them from scratch. Modern, fast, mobile-friendly, with online booking built in.

Looking at your current site, we noticed [specific issue 1], [specific issue 2], and [specific issue 3]. Those things are costing you customers every week — people land on the site on their phone, it doesn't load right, and they just go to your competitor.

The mockup we sent shows what your site could look like. Same business, same name, same story — just presented the way it deserves to be."

Discovery questions (5–12 min)

"Can I ask you a couple quick questions so I understand your situation better?"

Q1: "How are most of your new customers finding you right now — word of mouth, Google, Yelp, something else?"

Q2: "When someone does find you online, what do you want them to do — call you, walk in, book online?"

Q3: "Do you currently have any way for people to book or contact you online, or is it mostly phone?"

Q4: "Has your website ever been updated since it was first built, or is it pretty much the original?"

Q5: "What would a good month look like for you in terms of new customers?"
💡 Listen more than you talk here. Their answers tell you exactly how to frame the value. If they say "most people call us" — that's your opening to talk about how a modern site generates more calls. If they say "we want online booking" — you already have that built in.

The Pitch (12–16 min)

"Here's what we'd build for you. A clean, modern website — [4–5 pages depending on business type]. It loads fast on phones. It has a clear call to action on every page — either a booking button, a contact form, or a call button. And it's fully secure with HTTPS.

For [restaurant/barbershop/auto repair] specifically, we'd include [relevant feature — reservations / online booking / service menu].

We handle everything — design, copy, build, launch. You don't touch a thing. It takes about 2 weeks from start to launch.

The investment is $1,500 — one time. No monthly fees, no contracts. You own it outright."

The Close (16–20 min)

"Based on what you've told me, I think this is a pretty straightforward fit. The site you have now isn't doing the job — and for $1,500 you get something that works for the next 5–10 years.

How does that sound to you?"
💡 Stop talking after you ask. Let them respond. Silence is okay. Don't fill it.
If they say yes: "Great. I'll send you a simple one-page agreement and a link to pay a 50% deposit to get started. Once that's in, we'll book a 30-minute kickoff call to gather your content and we'll be live in 2 weeks."

If they need to think: "Totally fair. What's the main thing you'd want to think through?" [Listen, address it, then:] "Would it help if I sent you the mockup again along with a summary of everything we'd build? That way you have something to look at and share with whoever else is involved."
🛡️Rebuttal guide — common objections
"I already have a website."
"Absolutely — and I'm not saying it's bad. What I'm saying is that based on what we looked at, it's not doing everything it could for you. [Specific issue] means people are probably bouncing before they contact you. The question isn't whether you have a website — it's whether your website is working as hard as it should be."
"$1,500 is too expensive."
"I hear you. Let me put it in perspective — if a better website brought you just 2 or 3 extra customers a month, how long would it take to pay for itself? For most businesses we work with, it's less than a month. After that it's pure upside. And there are no monthly fees — you pay once and you're done."
"I don't have time for this right now."
"That's exactly why we built this the way we did. You don't need to do anything. We gather your info in one 30-minute call, and then we build everything. You review it once before we launch. That's it. Total time commitment from you — maybe an hour."
"I need to talk to my partner / spouse / business partner first."
"Totally makes sense — this is a business decision. Would it be helpful if I sent over a one-page summary of everything included so you have something to share with them? And if it makes sense, we can set up a quick call with both of you."
"Can you just update my current site instead of rebuilding it?"
"We looked at that option, and honestly, the issues with your current site go pretty deep — [specific issue]. Patching it would cost almost as much and you'd still end up with an old foundation. A clean rebuild gives you something solid that'll last 5–10 years. That's why we build from scratch."
"I tried a web company before and it was a disaster."
"I'm sorry to hear that — unfortunately it's more common than it should be. What happened?" [Listen.] "What we do differently is we keep it simple — a proven formula, a fixed price, a fixed timeline, and you see the design before we build anything. No surprises."
"Can I see examples of other sites you've built?"
"Absolutely — I'll send you a few examples right after this call. They're all in your industry so you can see exactly what we'd build for you."
"How do I know this will actually get me more customers?"
"Honest answer — I can't guarantee more customers, because that depends on a lot of things. What I can guarantee is that your site will no longer be turning people away. Right now people are finding you, landing on your site, and leaving because it's hard to use on a phone or they can't figure out how to contact you. We fix that."
"Do I own the website after?"
"100% — you own everything outright. The domain, the files, all of it. We don't lock you into anything. Once it's built, it's yours."
"What about hosting and maintenance?"
"Hosting is separate — it runs about $10–20/month depending on the provider, which we'll help you set up. Maintenance is optional — most of our sites are simple enough that you won't need much. But if you ever need updates, we're available at an hourly rate."

🏗️The WebRevive website formula — 5 pages

Every WebRevive site follows the same structure. Clean, fast, mobile-first. Adapted for each business type but built on the same proven skeleton.

Page 1
Home
Hero with strong headline + CTA button. 3 trust signals (Google rating, years in business, # of customers). Services overview. One testimonial. Footer with contact + hours.
Page 2
Services / Menu
Clear list of what they offer with brief descriptions. Prices where appropriate. Photos. CTA at bottom.
Page 3
About
Story of the business, owner photo if available, years in operation, what makes them different. Builds trust before the contact ask.
Page 4
Book / Contact
Online booking widget (Calendly, Booksy, OpenTable depending on category) OR simple contact form. Phone number prominent. Map embed. Hours.
Page 5
Reviews / Gallery
Embedded Google reviews or hand-picked testimonials. Photo gallery if relevant (restaurants, salons). Optional — skip for simpler businesses.
Add-on
AI Chatbot
Embedded on all pages. Answers FAQs, captures name/email/phone, books appointments. Powered by Claude API. $200–500 one-time.
